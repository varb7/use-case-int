# Privacy and Data Processing Agreement Terms

**Owner:** Legal. **Last reviewed:** 2025-09-18.

## Roles

For the Consent Management Platform, the customer is the controller and Usercentrics is the processor of end-user consent data. Usercentrics is the controller for its own customer account data (admin user names, emails, billing).

## Data Processing Agreement

A standard DPA is incorporated by reference into all contracts and is available online. The DPA covers Article 28 GDPR requirements: processing on documented instructions, confidentiality of personnel, security measures (Annex II), subprocessor terms, assistance with data subject rights and DPIAs, deletion or return at end of contract, and audit rights. Modifications to the standard DPA are negotiated on Enterprise contracts only and approved by Legal.

## Retention

Consent records are stored for 36 months to allow customers to demonstrate consent for the period in which enforcement action is typically possible. Customers may configure a shorter retention period on request.

## Data subject rights

As processor, Usercentrics assists customers in responding to data subject requests. End users can retrieve their consent record via the consent ID shown in the customer's privacy settings. Requests received directly by Usercentrics are forwarded to the relevant customer within 5 business days.

## Data Protection Officer

Usercentrics has appointed an external Data Protection Officer, reachable at dpo@. The DPO is registered with the Bavarian data protection authority (BayLDA).

## Liability

The standard terms cap Usercentrics' aggregate liability at the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim. Changes to liability caps, indemnities, and insurance requirements are negotiated by Legal on Enterprise contracts only.

## Insurance

Usercentrics maintains cyber liability and professional indemnity insurance. Coverage limits are confidential and shared with Legal approval only.
