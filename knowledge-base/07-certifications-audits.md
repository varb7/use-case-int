## Not held

Usercentrics does not hold ISO 27701, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, PCI DSS, HITRUST, FedRAMP, C5, or TISAX. Usercentrics does not process payment card data or protected health information and does not pursue those frameworks.

## Penetration testing

An independent third-party penetration test of the customer-facing applications and APIs is performed annually. An executive summary of the most recent report is available to customers under NDA. Critical and high findings are remediated within 30 days and retested. In addition, a public vulnerability disclosure programme is in place at security@ with a published policy and safe-harbour statement.

## Internal audit

ISMS internal audits are performed annually by the GRC Engineer against the full ISO 27001 Annex A control set, with results reported to the Security Steering Committee.

## Customer audit rights

Customers on Enterprise plans have contractual audit rights as set out in the DPA, exercisable once per year on 30 days notice, at the customer's cost, and satisfied in the first instance by provision of the SOC 2 report and ISO certificate.
