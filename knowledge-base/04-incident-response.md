# Incident Response

**Owner:** Security. **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-10.

## Process

The Incident Response Policy defines a five-phase process: detection, triage, containment, eradication and recovery, and post-incident review. Incidents are classified Sev 1 to Sev 4. A Sev 1 or Sev 2 incident triggers the incident commander rotation and a dedicated war room.

## Detection

Security telemetry from cloud audit logs, endpoint detection, and application logs is aggregated in the SIEM. Alerts are routed to the 24/7 on-call Security Engineer. Target time to acknowledge a Sev 1 alert is 15 minutes.

## Customer notification

For a confirmed personal data breach affecting customer data, Usercentrics as processor notifies the affected customer without undue delay and in any case within 48 hours of confirming the breach, so that the customer can meet its 72-hour obligation under GDPR Article 33. Notification goes to the customer's registered security contact and includes the nature of the breach, categories and approximate volume of data affected, likely consequences, and measures taken.

## Post-incident

Every Sev 1 and Sev 2 incident receives a written post-incident review within 10 business days, including root cause, timeline, and corrective actions tracked to closure. Customers affected by a Sev 1 incident receive a summary on request.

## Testing

The incident response plan is exercised at least annually through a tabletop exercise involving Security, Engineering, Legal, and Communications. The most recent tabletop was held in February 2026 and simulated a credential compromise of a third-party CI integration.

## History

Usercentrics has not had a reportable personal data breach in the last 36 months.
