# Subprocessors and Supplier Security

**Owner:** Legal (list), Security (assessment). **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-01.

## Current subprocessors

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location of processing |
|---|---|---|
| Google Cloud Platform | Hosting, storage, compute | EU (Belgium, Netherlands), US for customers on US region |
| Cloudflare | CDN, DDoS protection, WAF | Global edge, EU origin |
| Datadog | Infrastructure monitoring and logging (no personal data by design) | EU (Frankfurt) |
| Zendesk | Customer support ticketing | EU |
| Salesforce | CRM for customer account data (not end-user data) | EU |
| HubSpot | Marketing communication to customer contacts | EU |

## Changes to the list

Customers are notified of new subprocessors at least 30 days before the subprocessor begins processing, via the subprocessor page and email to the registered privacy contact. Customers may object on reasonable grounds within that window, as set out in the DPA.

## Supplier assessment

All suppliers that process customer data undergo a security assessment before onboarding and annually thereafter. The assessment reviews certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2), the supplier's DPA, breach notification terms, and data location. Suppliers are risk-tiered; Tier 1 suppliers (GCP, Cloudflare) receive an in-depth review including their audit reports.

## Data transfers

Transfers outside the EEA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) supplemented by a transfer impact assessment. For US-based subprocessors, Usercentrics additionally relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the subprocessor is certified.
